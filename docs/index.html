How It Works

Up and running in
under 2 minutes.

1
Download & Run
Download Intruely.exe (~74MB portable). No installer, no admin rights needed. Double-click and it opens instantly.
2
Get Your Free API Key
Visit aistudio.google.com, sign in with Google, and copy your free Gemini API key. Takes under 30 seconds.
3
Set Up Your Mode
Click your profile → Manage Modes. Paste your resume, interview prep notes, or any context you want the AI to reference in every answer.
4
Go Stealth & Start
Toggle Stealth Active — the window becomes invisible to all screen sharing tools. Press Ctrl + Enter during any meeting for instant AI answers.
