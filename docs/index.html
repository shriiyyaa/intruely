How It Works

Up and running in
under 2 minutes.

1
Download & Run
Download Intruely 1.0.0.exe (~77MB portable). No installer, no admin rights needed. Just double-click and it runs.
2
Get Your Free API Key
Visit aistudio.google.com, sign in with Google, and copy your free Gemini API key. Takes under 30 seconds.
3
Set Up Your Mode
Click your profile → Manage Modes. Paste your resume, interview prep notes, or any context you want the AI to reference in every answer.
4
Go Stealth & Start
Toggle Stealth Active — the app becomes invisible to screen sharing. Press Ctrl + Enter during any meeting to get instant, context-aware AI answers.
